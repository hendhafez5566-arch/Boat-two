# Bot Two product experience standards
Approved 2026-11-27. Audit each route, nested tab, dialog, and action.
- Compact headers, consistent cards, toolbar controls and readable typography. Meaningful icons and accessible names. Polished primary gradients; semantic status colors; recognizable channel marks. Respect responsive layouts and branding.
- Search large collections server-side with pagination; distinguish loading, errors, empty and stale data. No invented metrics or silent truncation.
- Close floating menus on outside click, Escape and navigation. Warn before discarding edits. Guard repeated mutations and confirm destructive actions. Transient success notices last about four seconds; actionable errors remain.
- Preserve data, supported workflows, tenant boundaries and permissions. Do not activate delivery or payments merely for testing. Record build, runtime, visual and functional test status separately.
- First scope: dashboard, contact growth, order links and subscription journey. Links to independent top-level sections are tracked separately. No section is fully verified until authenticated functional and visual checks pass.
