# Boat Two — demonstrable product scope

Use the separate portable synthetic stack, not customer records or the old Railway release. This document is an evidence-based demonstration scope, not a contractual service/SLA or declaration that every P0/P1/P2 requirement is closed.

|Show|Proof required|Boundary to disclose|
|---|---|---|
|Company setup and staff access|Register, save company settings, invite and accept through UI; owner/admin/agent role evidence|Synthetic demo identities; official invitation-email transport is separately gated|
|Website bot and data collection|Create a Webchat and bot, configure fields/connections, save/reopen, simulator, publish and actual visitor|Do not infer official WhatsApp/Meta/Telegram or provider AI acceptance|
|Inbox and customer continuity|Actual human handoff, agent reply, history reload and customer persisted in CRM|Assignment fairness/recovery evidence is separate from the full user journey|
|CRM and follow-up|Same visitor contact, real follow-up saved/reopened and visible to authorized staff|Previously accepted product/opportunity workflows remain scoped to their own evidence|
|Basic reporting|Counts and measured first-human-response from persisted synthetic events|Bot state is not proven resolution; measured time savings/financial benefit are unavailable, never invented|
|Portable secure attachments|Private filesystem/S3 adapters, unchanged tenant/lifecycle gate, no-store, actual independent replacement/recovery acceptance|Local S3 protocol acceptance is not acceptance of a particular cloud account; no current customer bytes were migrated|

For current CI IDs and accepted/unfinished connected-journey steps read PRODUCT_COMPLETION_20261118.md and the P0/P1/P2 progress files. No hosted public demo URL or working credentials are published here. A successful local setup retains its synthetic accounts in its own DB volume; CI destroys its disposable accounts after acceptance. Demo owner/agent credentials must remain in the private OS temporary file and be shared only through the operator's approved private channel.

External messaging, AI calls, email and subscription changes are off in the portable demo. Show unavailable provider-dependent capabilities as configuration/preparation only. No provider account, storage bill, production architecture, legal terms or support commitment was activated by this batch.
